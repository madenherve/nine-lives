How to play

Nine slots · one deck · no mercy

Nine cards are dealt face up. Pick any one, call what the next card off the deck will be, and try to burn through all 43 remaining cards before all nine slots are gone.

Your five calls

  • ▲ Higher — next card outranks the one you picked.
  • ▼ Lower — next card ranks below it.
  • = Same — next card matches its rank exactly.
  • Red — next card is hearts or diamonds.
  • Black — next card is spades or clubs.

Right and wrong

  • Correct: the drawn card replaces the one you called on.
  • Wrong: that slot burns and shows the card that killed it, greyed out.
  • All nine burned and you're done. Clear the deck and you win.

The Same comeback (house rule)

A correct Same call revives one burned slot — burned cards light up in brass, pick the one you want back. Roughly a 1-in-17 shot, so it's a bailout, not a plan.

Modes

  • Daily — one deal per day, identical for every player worldwide. Ace low, ties lose. Share your result when it's over.
  • Endless — replay freely, all rules adjustable, nothing shared.
  • Easy shows the counts panel and remembers burned cards. Hard hides every count and covers burned slots.

Keyboard

1–9 pick a slot, then H L S R B to call. Esc closes this.
